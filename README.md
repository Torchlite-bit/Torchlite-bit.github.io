# Torchlite's pages

The public pages of Torchlite's Aegis addons.

- [Aegis Instance Journal's Log Reader](https://torchlite-bit.github.io/Aegis_Forever_Instance_Journal/log-reader/):
  adds the boss spells from your combat log to a recording you copied from the journal's
  Record tab. Your log is read in your browser and never uploaded.

The addon itself is on [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/aegis-forever-instance-journal).
